# Code of conduct

Packman is a tool for people who package and deploy software. Everyone taking part in this project,
in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, is expected to follow this code.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas and code, not with people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming someone is wrong.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and fix them.
- Keep discussion about the project and what is best for the people using it.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address or email, without their permission.
- Discrimination or derogatory comments about age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
  characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
  status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers, using the contact details on the repository owner's
GitHub profile, or with GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or pull request.
Do not open a public issue for a conduct report. Reports are kept confidential, and the reporter's
identity is not shared without their consent.

Security vulnerabilities are reported separately; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainers review every report and respond in proportion to the behaviour: a private request to
stop, a public warning, removal of the content, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from the
project's spaces. Maintainers who break this code are held to the same consequences.
